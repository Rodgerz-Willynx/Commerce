Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, then choose “Order via WhatsApp” to discuss your order. The online checkout is a demo and does not yet process payments.

Where do you deliver?

MylesMart advertises delivery across Kenya. Delivery fees and estimated arrival times are not currently listed, so please confirm them when arranging your order.

Which payment methods can I use?

M-Pesa payment processing is not connected yet. Please confirm available payment options when arranging your order via WhatsApp.

How do I create an account or log in?

Create an account with your name, email, and password, then log in using the same email and password. Account information is currently saved only in this browser.

What if I forget my password?

Use “Forgot password?” on the login form to set a new password for the account email saved in this browser. This demo does not verify email ownership; do not use a password you rely on elsewhere.

Are the displayed ratings and stock quantities live?

No. Ratings and stock quantities are sample values for demonstration and are not connected to customer reviews or live inventory.

What is your returns policy?

Returns terms are not published yet. Please confirm the return or exchange conditions when arranging your order.
